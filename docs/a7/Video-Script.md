- [ ] Open `demo.html` in Chrome/Edge, press **F11** (full screen)
- [ ] Close all other tabs, browser downloads bar, and turn on "Do not disturb" (no pop-ups mid-take)
- [ ] Have your second module ready (open the file once more in a tab) in case a click goes wrong — restart with F5, takes 3 seconds

## Scene-by-scene script
**Format:** TIME = elapsed · SCREEN = what the audience sees · SAY = exactly what you say (read verbatim)

### 1. Introduction — 0:00 – 0:15 (welcome screen)
**SCREEN:** The welcome screen (title "Zero Incident"). Hold for 2 seconds, then click **Sign In**.

**SAY:**
> "Hi — this is the live prototype demonstration of the Zero Incident Safety Training Simulator, built by Team TechYeti for the CET257 Enterprise Project. The system trains warehouse employees using an interactive 3D environment. Let me walk you through the full training journey."

### 2. Sign In — 0:15 – 0:28 (login screen)
**SCREEN:** Login card with **ramesh.s / Welcome!2026 already typed in**. Click **Sign In** and let the short "Signing in…" beat play.

**SAY:**
> "First, the employee signs in. For the demo, the account is pre-filled — so I'll just click Sign In."

### 3. The 3D Training Hub — 0:28 – 0:55 (hub screen)
**SCREEN:** 3D warehouse hub visible. Move the mouse slowly over the trainer, then over each door. Do **not** open a door yet.

**SAY:**
> "After signing in, the employee lands in a walkable 3D warehouse — their training hub. Their 3D trainer welcomes them and guides the whole process. The three wooden doors are the three training modules: Hazard Perception, Manual Handling, and Working at Height. The progress bar at the bottom tracks completed modules."

### 4. Entering a module — 0:55 – 1:30 (module scene)
**SCREEN:** Click the **Manual Handling** door. Wait for the 360° scene. Then **drag slowly left–right** to show the scene is 360°. Point the mouse to marker **1** and click it.

**SAY:**
> "I'll open the Manual Handling module. The trainer briefs the task, and we're placed inside a 360-degree scene — I can look around the whole environment. The activity is checkpoint-based: I inspect each numbered marker and make a safe decision. This one is about using a handling aid instead of lifting by hand — so I choose that option."

### 5. Practical checkpoints — 1:30 – 1:50 (scene, checkpoints 2–3)
**SCREEN:** Click markers **2** and **3** in turn, each time picking the first (correct) option. Let the green "✔ Correct" feedback show briefly.

**SAY:**
> "Each correct decision is confirmed immediately, with an explanation of *why* it's the safe answer — so the learning sticks even before the quiz. I'll finish the last two checkpoints."

### 6. Timed quiz — 1:50 – 2:10 (quiz screen)
**SCREEN:** Quiz appears. Answer **question 1** (first option), let the timer bar run visibly for 2–3 seconds. Skip-forward feel: answer questions 2–4 quickly (all first options).

**SAY:**
> "The practical activity is followed by a short knowledge quiz — twenty seconds per question. Correct and incorrect answers both give instant feedback with the correct option highlighted."

### 7. Result & certificate — 2:10 – 2:38 (result → certificate)
**SCREEN:** Result screen showing the score (100/100 if all correct). Click **View Certificate**, let the certificate show, then click **Back to Training Hub** and show the door now marked **✓ Complete** and "Modules completed: 1 / 3".

**SAY:**
> "At the end, the system scores the attempt out of one hundred. Passing requires seventy. If the score is lower, the employee simply retakes the module — the best score is kept. On a pass, a completion certificate is issued, and the module is marked complete in the hub."

### 8. Closing — 2:38 – 2:50 (hub screen, hold)
**SCREEN:** Hold on the hub. End recording after the last word.

**SAY:**
> "That's the complete training journey — hub, 360-degree practical, timed quiz, scoring, retake, and certificate. The prototype validates the interaction and engagement concepts, with the full system continuing toward the remaining features. Thank you."

## Full narration in one block (teleprompter copy, ~410 words ≈ 2:50)
Hi — this is the live prototype demonstration of the Zero Incident Safety Training Simulator, built by Team TechYeti for the CET257 Enterprise Project. The system trains warehouse employees using an interactive 3D environment. Let me walk you through the full training journey.

First, the employee signs in. For the demo, the account is pre-filled — so I'll just click Sign In.

After signing in, the employee lands in a walkable 3D warehouse — their training hub. Their 3D trainer welcomes them and guides the whole process. The three wooden doors are the three training modules: Hazard Perception, Manual Handling, and Working at Height. The progress bar at the bottom tracks completed modules.

I'll open the Manual Handling module. The trainer briefs the task, and we're placed inside a 360-degree scene — I can look around the whole environment. The activity is checkpoint-based: I inspect each numbered marker and make a safe decision. This one is about using a handling aid instead of lifting by hand — so I choose that option.

Each correct decision is confirmed immediately, with an explanation of why it's the safe answer — so the learning sticks even before the quiz. I'll finish the last two checkpoints.

The practical activity is followed by a short knowledge quiz — twenty seconds per question. Correct and incorrect answers both give instant feedback with the correct option highlighted.

At the end, the system scores the attempt out of one hundred. Passing requires seventy. If the score is lower, the employee simply retakes the module — the best score is kept. On a pass, a completion certificate is issued, and the module is marked complete in the hub.

That's the complete training journey — hub, 360-degree practical, timed quiz, scoring, retake, and certificate. The prototype validates the interaction and engagement concepts, with the full system continuing toward the remaining features. Thank you.

## Recording tips
- **Audio:** use a quiet room; speak 30 cm from the mic/laptop. Do a 10-second test recording first and listen back.
- **If the score comes out under 70** (a misclick): it's actually a *feature* — pause and say "as you can see, below seventy the system offers a retake" and retake once. But aiming for a clean pass is safer.
- **Timer bar:** don't wait for it to drain — answer as soon as you finish speaking the line.
- **Cut point:** stop recording on the hub after "Thank you", then trim the last 1 second of silence in your editor.
- **Export:** 1080p, MP4. If your tool has captions, add them — markers help graders follow the steps.
- File name suggestion: `CET257_TechYeti_LivePrototypeDemo.mp4`
